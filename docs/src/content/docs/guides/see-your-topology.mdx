Describe your environment, and see how stormbreaker fits on it: what it adds, where a run's load goes, and what each team sets up. Your services do not change. Stormbreaker adds an orchestrator, a dispatcher beside each service that takes part, and mocks for the dependencies you choose to mock, inside your environment or outside it.
